# Trademark policy

The Emergi Foundation uses the following marks in connection with
its projects:

- **Emergi**, **Emergi Foundation** (Foundation identity)
- **emburden** (analytical software ecosystem)

Registration status: as of 2026-09-19, none of the marks are
formally registered with a trademark office. Registration with
usPTO (~$250–350 per mark) is on the Foundation's roadmap; this
policy applies to unregistered use in the interim.

## Permitted use (fair use)

Anyone may use the marks to:

- **Accurately refer** to Emergi Foundation projects, e.g. "built
  with `emburden`", "cite as (Emergi Foundation, 2026)".
- **Cite** the Foundation as a project's steward or publisher in
  academic work, journalism, or educational content.
- **Link** to Foundation projects and websites.
- **Fork** and modify Foundation open-source projects under their
  respective licenses (typically AGPL-3.0-or-later), including
  keeping the project's original name for the unmodified version.

## Restricted use

The following require the Foundation's written permission:

- **Modified forks** of a Foundation project distributed under the
  Foundation's project names (rename your fork to signal
  divergence, e.g. `emburden-<my-org>` or an unrelated name).
- **Commercial products or services** that use "Emergi" or
  "emburden" in their name, branding, or marketing in a way that
  implies endorsement or affiliation.
- **Domain names** incorporating the marks (`emburden-*.com`,
  `emergi-*.org`, etc.).
- **Merchandise** using the marks.

## Endorsement

Use of the marks does not imply Foundation endorsement of any
particular company, product, or service. The Foundation does not
endorse commercial offerings that build on its open-source
projects.

## Attribution

When using the marks in fair-use contexts, prefer:

- "the emburden ecosystem" or "emburden" (the project name)
- "Emergi Foundation" (the steward entity, when referring to
  publication or citation)
- "Emrgi" (the umbrella brand, less common in academic contexts)

## Enforcement

Trademark-related questions and permission requests go to
`info@emrgi.org`. The Foundation reserves the right to enforce its
rights in the marks under applicable trademark law once formal
registration is completed.
