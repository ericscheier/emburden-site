Ecological Economics — Research Article

Where does emergy accounting give new information? A systematic cross-country evaluation of 36 emergy-native metrics against 40 mainstream outcomes

Status: v9 draft, ~11,000 words, PDF re-rendered. Systematic ΔR² evaluation across a 1,520-cell grid; Odum-tradition claims tested against modern data; Energy Adequacy framing threaded through household EROI ↔︎ HEII quantile spread ↔︎ societal NER ↔︎ MEP (Q3.18). Six falsification steps run on the Q3.16-E2 renewables → life-expectancy puzzle; Q3.24 closes it (demographic transition, −0.52 years per pp elderly share, p = 2.2e-9). Q3.34 extends the demographic control across the full 918-cell grid: 288/309 significant findings (93%) survive robust. Q3.33 Kigali F-gas amendment + Q3.35A oil-price shocks form the paper’s natural-experiment pair (external policy shock × external price shock). Q3.35B projects the alpha grid onto native UN SDG indicators; Q3.35C projected onto a Doughnut-Economics country snapshot (initial 17-dimension construction, since superseded). Q3.36 + Q3.37 extend the framework outward into finance and close the Doughnut: (a) capital-market alpha — sovereign debt levels are predicted by sector_industrial (ΔR² = 0.219, p = 3e-8) and by heii_norm_p10 on debt-service burden (ΔR² = 0.177, p = 8e-4); equity outcomes show weaker alpha (boundary claim: emergy captures physical-provisioning that debt finances, not forward growth options); (b) the full 21-dimension Doughnut (12 social-floor + 9 ecological-ceiling) with continuous signed distance-from-boundary; (c) cross-benchmark validation of the Q3.35A oil-shock finding across Brent, Dubai, WTI, average (all +0.27 to +0.29 coefficients, p < 5e-3). Q3.43 names the mechanism: UPBEAT (World Bank / ESMAP) + LAC-UB + ESMAP RISE + AfDB ERI harmonised into one 39,000-row country-year utility + regulatory panel, z-normalised within (source, indicator). heii_norm_p90 predicts utility Collection Rate at ΔR² = +0.73 (p = 7e-12, N = 52) — utility financial stress is the intermediate mechanism the Q3.36A sovereign-debt result pointed at. Q3.44B confirms the direction: within-country fixed-effect panel with 2-year cross-lag shows HEII_norm at t-2 forward-predicts Collection Rate at t (p = 0.036, N = 361, 52 countries), while the reverse lag is null (p = 0.81). Q3.44C extends to Afrobarometer R9+R10 microdata (39 countries × 9 items, all publicly downloadable): HEII_norm_p90 carries alpha only on lived_no_electricity (ΔR² = +0.23, p = 0.012), not on the four other lived-poverty items nor the perception items — HEII is a specifically-electricity signal, mediation is mechanical not cultural. Q3.46B robustness: swapping the GDP-per-capita baseline for household private consumption (WB NE.CON.PRVT.PC.KD) survives across 3,117 refitted cells — 235 findings survive or strengthen, 73 weaken or become fragile, 46 new findings emerge under the cleaner baseline. The flagship collection_rate ← heii_norm cell nearly doubles (ΔR² = +0.37 → +0.71) under the consumption baseline; consumption is now the paper’s preferred specification. Q3.46C identification: with a Gini-implied log-normal multiplier on the annual HEII panel, the P90 rung enters the cross-lag directly — forward p = 5×10⁻⁵ (N = 356, 51 countries), reverse null (p = 0.92). Direction of causality identified within country and over time.

Reproduction: 12 scripts wired into a documented pipeline; every figure and table is regeneratable from the tagged commit.

Next: external co-author read + finalise which corresponding- author byline lands. No analytical blockers.

Publication strategy

Each paper answers a different question at a different scale, so a single methodological debate can’t unravel the whole stack. Reviewers at each stage inherit peer-reviewed context from the layer below.
  • JOSS package papers — package-level DOIs for every dependency
  • JSS umbrella — architectural rationale; downstream papers cite it
  • Scientific Data — dataset itself as a citable object
  • Joule — validated tool + data applied to a global synthesis
  • Science Policy Forum — 1,500-word policy-actionable condensation
  • Ecological Economics — the emergy methodological question, isolated
  • Nature Energy — causal-identification argument built on the validated dataset

What’s actually blocking what

  • ORCID blocks four items: Ecological Economics byline, Science Policy Forum submission, JOSS Wave 1 metadata, JSS authorship. Registration is free and takes ~15 minutes at orcid.org/register. This is the single cheapest unlock in the queue.
  • EM-DAT / GDELT / IHME GBD credentials block Nature Energy. Each is a separate access request; expect 2-4 weeks per institution.
  • Zenodo record upload blocks Scientific Data (and populates emburdensynth/R/global_data_download.R:20-24).
  • External co-author read is the last-mile blocker on Ecological Economics and Joule — analytically complete, waiting on eyes.

Everything else is writing time.
